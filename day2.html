CONTENT TO INCOME · DAY TWO

I learned the strategy.
Then I turned it into content.

Day Two wasn’t just about learning another content framework. The assignment was to identify my biggest takeaway, turn it into a concept, and actually create the video.

The lesson: content needs different jobs.

THE HOMEWORK

My biggest takeaway: the three levels of content.

The framework helped me stop thinking of every post as if it needed to accomplish the same thing. Some content should connect. Some should teach. Some should lead toward an offer.

01

EVERYBODY CONTENT

Connect first.

Concept

The Soft Pour

Relatable coffee moments, everyday conversations, funny mistakes, routines, opinions, and real-life connection.

02

EDUCATE CONTENT

Teach something useful.

Concept

Build With Me

Showing women how I use AI, digital tools, strategy, and what I am learning while building my own next chapter.

03

EARN CONTENT

Create a useful offer.

Concept

Next Chapter Toolkit

Future planners, templates, tools, and resources designed to help women put their own ideas into action.

FROM LESSON TO EXECUTION

I didn't just explain the concept.
I created the video.

My assignment was to share my biggest takeaway from Content to Income, explain the three levels of content, and demonstrate how I could turn that lesson into something creative using AI.

  1. 01

    THE TAKEAWAY

    Identified the three levels of content: Everybody, Educate, and Earn. The lesson was that different content serves different purposes.

  2. 02

    THE CONCEPT

    Decided to create a short-form video using a realistic AI version of myself to explain the lesson in a way that felt personal and connected to my brand.

  3. 03

    THE SCRIPT & AUDIO

    Developed the explanation and prepared a voice track approximately 49 seconds long.

  4. 04

    THE AI VIDEO EXPERIMENT

    Used an AI video-generation workflow with a reference character image and attempted to produce natural speaking movements and expressions.

  5. 05

    TESTING & LIMITATIONS

    Reviewed the generation cost, duration limitations, movement quality, and consistency of the AI character. The output was not perfect, but I evaluated what worked and what needed improvement.

  6. 06

    THE FINAL DECISION

    Chose to use the existing video rather than keep spending credits regenerating it. The experience became part of the lesson: creating with AI still requires judgment, iteration, and budget awareness.

THE TAKEAWAY

I didn't just learn about content.
I learned how to create with purpose.

My biggest takeaway from Content to Income was understanding that content has three different jobs: Everybody content builds connection, Educate content provides value, and Earn content creates opportunities for income.

I used that framework to develop content concepts for my Jaida Mayrie brand and turned the homework into an AI-generated video.

Creating the video also taught me about working with AI tools, managing credits, evaluating results, and knowing when to stop regenerating and work with what I already had.

The biggest lesson was that AI can help bring an idea to life, but I still have to make the creative decisions.

From learning the strategy to creating the content — that's intentional reinvention in action.
