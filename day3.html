FROM PROMPT TO PRODUCTION · DAY THREE

I had the vision.
Then I learned how to prompt it.

Day Three was about moving beyond experimenting with AI and learning how to communicate a creative vision. I explored how prompts, visual references, movement instructions, and revisions shape the final video.

WHAT I LEARNED

What I Learned

Learning how specific instructions influence the results.

Understanding how reference images help maintain a recognizable character.

Learning to describe natural speaking movements, gestures, and expressions.

Evaluating results and refining instructions instead of accepting every generation.

THE CREATION

From the prompt to the final video.

After learning how to communicate my creative vision through AI prompts, I put those lessons into practice by creating my own video. This project allowed me to experiment with visual consistency, realistic movement, and creative direction.

Every prompt was a chance to learn. Every revision helped me understand how to communicate my vision more clearly. The goal wasn't just to generate a video — it was to learn how to direct the creative process.
